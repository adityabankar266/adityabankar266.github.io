Automating distributor claim settlement

The context

In FMCG, distributors fund trade promotions first and claim the money back from the brand. Until a claim is settled, the distributor is lending the brand money. On-invoice schemes leave a trail in the invoice. Off-invoice schemes, like display incentives, need proof such as photos.

On my last DMS rollout I mapped as-is and to-be processes for promotions and claims and configured six promotion types. This concept applies AI and automation to the settlement steps that stay manual in most set-ups.

The problem

Most of the settlement time goes on manual checking, not decisions:

  • Claims are compiled by hand from invoices and paper proof.
  • Display and visibility claims depend on photos that someone checks one by one.
  • Sales and finance check every line, routine or not.
  • Disputed lines hold up the whole claim, and the distributor's money with it.

What I did

  • Mapped the claim journey for on-invoice and off-invoice schemes from my project experience.
  • Designed the automated steps: claim generation, rule checks and AI photo checks.
  • Defined the validation rules and what counts as an exception.
  • Wrote user stories with Gherkin acceptance criteria.

The approach

  1. 1
    Build claims from invoicesOn-invoice claims are generated from invoiced sales in the DMS, so the evidence is already in the system.
  2. 2
    Check photos with AIFor off-invoice schemes, AI checks each proof photo for the right display, shop and date, and flags doubtful ones.
  3. 3
    Validate by ruleRules check eligibility, budget, validity dates and duplicates on every line.
  4. 4
    Review by exceptionOnly lines that break a rule or fail the photo check go to a reviewer, with the reason shown.
  5. 5
    Connect to the ERPApproved claims create a credit note in SAP without re-keying.

Sample work: user story

Story · AI03-US-04

Flag doubtful display photos

As a claims reviewer, I want display photos to be checked automatically against the scheme, so that I only look at the doubtful ones.

Acceptance criteria

  • Given a display claim line has a photo, when the check runs, then the line is marked 'Photo passed' or 'Photo doubtful' with the reason.
  • Given a photo is dated outside the scheme period, when the check runs, then the line is marked 'Photo doubtful: wrong date'.
  • Given the same photo is used for two shops, when the check runs, then both lines are flagged as possible duplicates.
  • Given a line is marked 'Photo doubtful', when I open the claim, then I see the photo, the reason and the options to approve or reject.

Expected outcomes

  • Distributors get reimbursed faster, because routine claims need no manual checks.
  • Reviewers spend their time only on doubtful photos and rule breaks.
  • Fewer duplicate or invalid claims get paid.
  • Every decision has a recorded reason.

Design principles

  • Most claim delays start at promotion set-up, so fix that first.
  • Review by exception works only if finance and sales agree the rules before build.
  • AI flags, people decide.
