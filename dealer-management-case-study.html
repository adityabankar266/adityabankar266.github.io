One dealer management system for a tractor maker's 500+ dealers

The context

Tractor dealers do far more than sell. They handle enquiries and demos, order machines and parts from the manufacturer, keep stock, bill customers and service tractors for years after the sale. When each of those runs in a separate tool, nobody has the full picture.

At The Opal Labs I was the business analyst for a Dealer Management System and billing system for a global tractor manufacturer's network of 500+ dealers.

The problem

The platform had to bring a dealer's whole business into one place:

  • Pre-sales, sales, procurement, inventory and service needed to work as one system.
  • Requirements came from many business teams and had to be written so developers could build them.
  • The system had to exchange data with the manufacturer's systems through APIs.
  • Reports were slow to produce.

My role

  • Gathered requirements across the pre-sales, sales, procurement, service and marketing modules.
  • Translated them into BRD and SRS documents, including data and API integration requirements.
  • Worked with technical teams and business stakeholders through delivery.
  • Optimised report generation, cutting report turnaround time by about 50%.

Results

  • 500+ dealers on one Dealer Management System and billing system.
  • Pre-sales, sales, procurement, inventory and service in one place.
  • Report turnaround cut by about 50%.
  • Requirements documented end to end in BRD and SRS.

What I learned

  • One source of truth matters more than any single feature.
  • Write integration requirements early. Scale problems are expensive to fix late.
  • Reports get used when they answer a planner's actual question.
