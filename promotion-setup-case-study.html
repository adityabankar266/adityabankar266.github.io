From promotion brief to live scheme, without re-keying

The context

Every trade promotion starts as a brief from the brand team: which products, which shops, what discount or free goods, what budget and for how long. Someone then has to set it up in the DMS, field by field.

On my last DMS rollout I configured and tested six promotion types, on-invoice and off-invoice, for two markets. This concept uses that experience to design an AI-assisted set-up flow.

The problem

Set-up is manual, and mistakes surface only on invoices:

  • Briefs arrive as PDFs and emails, worded differently each time.
  • Each promotion is keyed into the DMS by hand.
  • Overlapping schemes and wrong dates are found only when invoices go wrong.
  • Corrections reach distributors and salesmen late.

What I did

  • Listed the fields each of the six promotion types needs, from my configuration and testing work.
  • Designed the extraction, validation and approval steps.
  • Defined conflict rules for overlaps, budgets, dates and eligibility.
  • Wrote a field-mapping sample and acceptance criteria for the draft step.

The approach

  1. 1
    Standardise the briefOne brief template per promotion type, so the assistant always knows what to look for.
  2. 2
    Extract into a draftThe AI assistant reads the brief and fills a draft set-up: type, products, shops, value, budget and dates.
  3. 3
    Check before anyone approvesRules flag overlapping schemes, missing budgets, invalid dates and products not on the price list.
  4. 4
    Keep a person in chargeThe brand or trade marketing owner reviews and approves every draft. Nothing goes live on the AI's word alone.
  5. 5
    Publish onceThe approved promotion goes to the DMS and the salesman app together, so both show the same scheme.

Expected outcomes

  • Faster set-up, because nobody types promotion fields in by hand.
  • Fewer wrong discounts reaching invoices.
  • Distributors and salesmen see the same scheme on the same day.
  • A clear approval record for every promotion.

Design principles

  • Standardise the input before automating it.
  • AI drafts, people approve.
  • Catch conflicts before launch. Fixing invoices costs far more.
