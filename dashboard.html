Design choices

  • Headline figures cover one month and one region, never a sum across months.
  • The time axis runs in calendar order. Category charts and the table are sorted by value.
  • One number style throughout: Indian grouping with ₹ lakh and crore.
  • Red appears only where someone needs to act: claims older than 30 days and figures below target.
