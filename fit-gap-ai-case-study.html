Using GenAI to check 200+ global user stories for market fit

Concept case study · 2026 · Based on my fit-gap and Key Design Decision work on a DMS rollout

ConceptA design based on my project experience, written during my 2026 sabbatical. It is not a deployed client solution.

The context

When a global company rolls its template system out to a new market, someone has to check every global user story against that market's rules. On my last DMS rollout I reviewed 200+ global user stories for fit in two markets, then wrote gap analysis and Key Design Decision (KDD) documents for business sign-off.

That review is careful, repetitive work. I already used Microsoft Copilot to speed up analysis and documentation on that project. This concept designs a more structured, repeatable version.

The problem

Fit-gap review is slow, and most of it repeats for every market:

  • Every story is read by hand against tax, invoicing and legal rules for each market.
  • Gaps, reasons and decisions sit in spreadsheets that drift out of date.
  • KDDs and localisation stories are written from scratch each time.
  • The same review starts again for every new market.

What I did

  • Built the review approach from my own fit-gap and KDD work.
  • Designed a market rule library and the assistant's output format.
  • Defined review steps so the BA owns every decision.
  • Wrote a sample of the assistant's output and the BA's decisions.

The approach

  1. 1
    Build a market rule libraryWrite each market's rules for tax, invoice and credit-note numbering and legal reporting as plain sentences.
  2. 2
    Compare story by storyThe assistant compares each global story with the rule library and suggests fit, partial fit or gap.
  3. 3
    Quote the reasonEvery suggestion quotes the rule behind it, so the BA can check it in seconds.
  4. 4
    Let the BA decideThe BA accepts, edits or rejects each suggestion. Nothing is final until the BA decides.
  5. 5
    Draft the paperworkFor each agreed gap, the assistant drafts a KDD and a localisation story with Gherkin acceptance criteria for the BA to finish.
  6. 6
    Reuse for the next marketThe rule library and past decisions carry over, so the next rollout starts ahead.

Expected outcomes

  • A faster first pass through large global backlogs.
  • Every fit and gap decision recorded with its reason.
  • KDDs and localisation stories start from a consistent draft.
  • The next market rollout starts from what was already decided.

Design principles

  • The assistant suggests; the BA decides.
  • Quote the rule, or the suggestion is useless.
  • Write the rules in plain sentences first. AI can only check what is written down.
